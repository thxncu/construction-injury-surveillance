# Source provenance

- Source system: Construction Safety Management Integrated Information (CSI), operated by the Korea Authority of Land and Infrastructure Safety (KALIS).
- Source export date: June 30, 2025.
- Observation period: July 1, 2019, through June 30, 2025 (37,196 reported construction incidents).
- Shared representation: de-identified analytic extract limited to the structured variables used in the analyses (23 columns including an anonymous identifier). See `data_dictionary.csv` (in `outputs/`) and `VALUE_LABELS.csv`.
- Disclosure control: see `DATA_PRIVACY_NOTE.md`.
- Not redistributed: free-text fields, direct identifiers, and fields not used in the analyses.
- Access to the original register: through KALIS, subject to its terms.
